      About, copyright and privacy

      Copyright. © 2026 jnanendray. All rights reserved for this app's own code, design and icons. News belongs to the publishers named on each story. Their names and trademarks belong to them and are used only to credit the source.

      Sources. Stories are gathered from Indian news channels and newspapers, and Karnataka stories from the state's local channels and newspapers. Each story names its sources. Stories marked Important were reported recently and matter to many people.

      Content. Each story is a short summary written in original words by an AI from public news reports. No photographs, full articles or paid content are copied. Each story links to where you can find the original report. Kannada text is machine-written, so check the original for exact wording. Summaries can contain mistakes and are not legal, medical or financial advice.

      Privacy. No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your language choice and recently viewed briefings are stored only on your device, and a briefing you have not opened for 24 hours is deleted from it. Speech comes from your device's own text-to-speech. Links open in the in-app reader and those sites have their own policies.

      Removal requests. To report an error or ask for any item to be removed, open an issue at github.com/jnanendray/daily_news. Version 2.0, see CHANGELOG.md.
