# Copyright, content and privacy

Copyright (c) 2026 jnanendray. All rights reserved for this project's own code, design and icons.

**News content.** News stories belong to the publishers credited on each story. This project shows short summaries written in original words by an AI from public news reports, credits each source, and links to where the original report can be found. It does not copy photographs, full articles or paid content. Publisher names and trademarks belong to their owners and are used only to credit sources.

**Accuracy.** Summaries and Kannada text are machine-written and can contain mistakes. They are not legal, medical or financial advice. Check the original report.

**Privacy.** No accounts, ads, analytics, cookies or tracking. The page makes no third-party requests. Language choice and recently opened briefings are stored only on the device, and a briefing not opened for 24 hours is deleted from it. The Android app asks only for the INTERNET permission. Speech is produced by the device's own text-to-speech; the app may ask Android to download a voice (for example Kannada) through the system's own installer. Links open in an in-app reader; those sites have their own policies.

**Removal and corrections.** Open an issue at https://github.com/jnanendray/daily_news/issues to report an error or ask for any item to be removed.

This notice is not legal advice. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

**Notifications.** The Android app can show a daily reminder only if you switch it on, and only after Android asks for your permission. No data is collected or sent.
